A fantasy villain's monologue of about 100 words, spoken on the night of the
47th siege of Aldermere. He has the trebuchets, the ravens, and his enemy's
steward's signed confession — and a kettle left on, a tower about to burn, and
a contract waiting on page four. His exhaustion never gets stated outright:
it leaks through the deflections, the trailing sentences, and the soup.

`main.py` writes the monologue to `/output/entry.txt` and prints it with its
word count. A `validate_entry()` gate enforces about 100 words (90–115) and
rejects any flat statement of weariness, so the tiredness can only live in
the voice.
